# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Super Admin** (platform owner) — manages all tenants, global AI defaults, billing, modules.
- **Director / Org Admin** — owns everything inside their org; lives in the Command Centre.
- **Estimator** — works the tender pipeline.
- **Project Manager** — manages programs, labour, deliveries, allocation.
- **Site Foreman** — mobile-only; submits daily updates from the job site, often one-handed with gloves on, in outdoor/glare conditions.
- **Viewer / Management** — read-only across projects.

Context: Paracon Group, a high-end commercial fitout firm in North Melbourne ("Build in Parallel"), is tenant #1 of a multi-tenant SaaS being commercialised for the broader fitout industry. Office roles work on desktop/laptop; foremen work on a phone outdoors.

## Product Purpose

Paracon OS is a construction operating system: tender pipeline → projects/program → labour intelligence → forecast & capacity → allocation → site daily updates → productivity feedback loop, with an AI layer throughout. Success looks like: a director sees project health and capacity at a glance, a PM staffs projects without surprises, and a foreman completes a full daily update in under 5 minutes one-handed.

## Brand Personality

Voice: precise, calm, confident — echoes "Build in Parallel." The product should feel unique, professional, animated, and high-end — like a crafted, expensive product — never a templated admin panel.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard clichés: bento grids, gradient-text metric cards, identical card grids, the hero-metric template.
- Consumer/playful, gamified UI patterns — this is professional B2B software for a premium fitout firm.
- Dense, dated enterprise/legacy-ERP screens despite the B2B context.
- Anything that reads as templated or AI-generated rather than bespoke and deliberately crafted.

## Design Principles

1. **Build in Parallel** — the product's value prop is running parallel projects off one source of truth; the UI should make state legible at a glance, never ambiguous.
2. **Calm precision over noise** — RAG colours and the brass accent are used sparingly and meaningfully (forecast/risk, active states, key figures), never decoratively.
3. **Mobile-first for the field, desktop-dense for the office** — foreman screens optimise for one-handed, gloved, outdoor use; director/PM screens can carry more information density.
4. **Config, not code** — admin-editable settings (AI provider, statuses, thresholds) should feel like a real control panel, not exposed engineering knobs.
5. **Premium craft, not template** — motion, type, and colour should feel bespoke and high-end; never the default AI-dashboard scaffold.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline, verified by precise contrast-ratio computation rather than eyeballing. Foreman-facing mobile screens carry extra weight: 48px minimum tap targets, one-handed usability, and contrast margins held above the AA minimum (not right at it) given outdoor/glare use.
